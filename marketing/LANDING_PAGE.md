# Cosmic Synthesizer Engine

**Precision at the Core. Character at the Edges.**

A reusable DSP foundation for the next generation of analog-inspired VST instruments.

Inspired by the precision engineering of CMOS RF and mm-Wave oscillators and frequency synthesizers — translated into original, real-time audio algorithms.

### What you get
- Rock-solid phase accumulation
- Shared-phase quadrature oscillators
- Nonlinear tuning curves
- Deterministic vintage drift
- Soft phase-locking & frequency division
- Header-only C++20 core + JUCE starter

### What you don’t get (yet)
- Finished multi-voice synthesizer
- Production preset library
- Marketing hype numbers

Built for developers who care about the difference between “sounds okay” and “feels right”.
