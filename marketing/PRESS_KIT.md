# Press Kit

**Name:** Cosmic Synthesizer Engine  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Type:** Open-source C++20 DSP library + JUCE instrument starter  
**License:** MIT  

**One-liner:** Precision phase-accurate oscillators with optional analog character for serious synthesizer design.

**Boilerplate:**  
Cosmic Synthesizer Engine is a modular audio DSP foundation that brings high-resolution phase accumulation, exact quadrature generation, controllable nonlinear tuning and musical phase-locking techniques into the world of VST instrument development. Its architecture is inspired by engineering principles from CMOS RF and mm-Wave oscillator and synthesizer design. Released as research-grade source code under the MIT license.
