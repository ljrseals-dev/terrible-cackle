# Social Launch Copy

**Short**  
Cosmic Synthesizer Engine 0.1.0 is out — a clean C++20 DSP core for phase-accurate, character-rich synthesizer oscillators. Precision at the core. Character at the edges. Inspired by RF/mm-Wave synthesizer engineering principles.

**Thread starter**  
Most digital oscillators are either sterile or randomly noisy.  
We wanted something better: mathematically stable phase, exact quadrature, and *optional*, reproducible analog-style drift and nonlinear tuning.  

The architecture draws inspiration from the precision techniques used in CMOS RF and mm-Wave frequency synthesizers — adapted for real-time audio.

Cosmic Synthesizer Engine 0.1.0 is the first public foundation. Header-only, real-time safe, MIT licensed.
