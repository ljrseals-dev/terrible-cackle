# Product Brief — Cosmic Synthesizer Engine 0.1.0

A modular C++20 DSP library and JUCE starter for building high-quality analog-inspired synthesizer instruments.

Core strengths:
- 64-bit phase-accurate oscillators
- Exact quadrature generation
- Controllable nonlinear tuning and drift
- Musical PLL-style synchronization
- Clean digital mode + character modes

Engineering foundation inspired by the stability, phase-control and synthesizer techniques discussed in modern CMOS RF / mm-Wave literature.

Target users: instrument designers, DSP researchers, modular synthesizer developers.

Status: research / foundation release. Not yet a finished commercial product.
