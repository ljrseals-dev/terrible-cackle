# Brand

**Product:** Cosmic Synthesizer Engine  
**Tagline:** Precision at the Core. Character at the Edges.

**Inspiration**  
Engineering principles drawn from CMOS RF and mm-Wave oscillator and frequency-synthesizer design, adapted for real-time audio DSP.  
Reference work (inspiration only): *CMOS RF and mm-Wave Transceivers and Synthesizers* (Thangarasu, Mahalingam, Ma, Yeo — Jenny Stanford Publishing).

**Visual language**
- Cosmic black backgrounds
- Electric violet & neon magenta accents
- Signal cyan indicators
- Oscilloscope / orbital phase graphics
- Laboratory instrumentation meets modular synthesizer aesthetics

**Tone**  
Professional, technically credible, slightly futuristic.  
Clearly distinguishes implemented features from planned ones.  
Never invents benchmarks, testimonials or hardware claims.
